This is a personal portfolio and technical blog. You can read it without creating an account or submitting a form.

## Site analytics

The production site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and how they navigate the site. Analytics may use first-party cookies and collect page views, referral information, and browser and device information. See [Google's explanation of Analytics cookies](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The site's analytics code disables Google signals and advertising personalization signals. It does not load Google Analytics when your browser exposes an enabled **Global Privacy Control** or **Do Not Track** signal. You can also block analytics with your browser's tracking protection or [Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Hosting and fonts

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Google Fonts supplies the Roboto Slab typeface. Your browser contacts these services when it loads the site and the font; those requests expose connection information such as your IP address. Their handling of that information is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Contact and external links

Email links open your email application. If you email me, I receive the information you choose to include. Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.
